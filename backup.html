UNDERSTAND YOUR CAPACITY

Storage counts every saved version.

Your plan covers the combined size of all encrypted restore points. A 10 GB archive saved seven times needs approximately 70 GB of storage.

Backups currently upload the full file each time. Incremental backup and deduplication are not included. GB = 1 billion bytes; 1 TB = 1,000 GB.

PLANNED BACKUP POLICY

Daily backups. Clear retention.

Our proposed schedule is once daily, with a seven-day retention window and at least one latest restore point kept. The schedule and retention process must be agreed and configured before service starts.

Expired backups are currently flagged for review, not automatically deleted. If capacity is reached, new backups stop while existing backups are kept.

Before you get started

What can I back up?

Linux files or archives you prepare. Automatic folder collection, live database consistency and whole-server recovery are not included. Database dumps and custom integrations require a separate scope and quote.

Who holds the encryption key?

You keep your encryption key separately from your backups. ELLION cannot decrypt your files if your key is lost. Keep a secure copy and verify a restore before relying on the service.

What help is included?

General setup and file restore guidance are proposed as part of the monthly plan. Confirm support hours and scope in your quote. Remote server recovery, 24/7 support and guaranteed recovery times are not included.

Is this a disaster recovery service?

No geographic redundancy or whole-server disaster recovery guarantee is included. Independent replication and recovery objectives need a separate agreement.

How do I start?

Choose a plan and email us your data size, backup frequency and restore needs. We confirm capacity, transfer allowance and terms before you pay. Do not send passwords, private keys or customer files by email.
