Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how ELLION ("we", "us") handles personal information when you use https://elliongpu.com and request a bare-metal GPU server.

1. Information we collect

  • Request details you provide: email address, name or organization, preferred login ID, intended use, rental period, GPU count and any message you write.
  • Technical data collected automatically: IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, and advertising and analytics identifiers set by cookies.

2. How requests are sent

When you press the request or reservation button, your email application opens with a pre-filled draft addressed to help@elliongpu.com. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. We receive only what you choose to send.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your request and agree on price, term and server configuration.
  • To provision and support the server you rent, and to arrange payment (PayPal or bank transfer).
  • To measure site traffic and the performance of our advertising.
  • To prevent abuse and meet legal or accounting obligations.

4. Cookies and third-party services

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads to measure visits and advertising. These services set cookies and receive technical data such as your IP address. We also load fonts and style files from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, and read server availability and pricing from Google Apps Script, which means your IP address is visible to those providers. Our email is handled by our email provider, and payments are handled by PayPal or your bank.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google's measurement using Google's Analytics opt-out add-on and Ads Settings. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the site, email, advertising measurement and payments, and where required by law. Providers may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States and South Korea.

6. Retention

We keep request emails and account details while you are a customer and for up to 12 months after the service ends, unless a longer period is required for accounting or legal reasons. Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics retention settings.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing help@elliongpu.com. Depending on where you live (for example California or other U.S. states, or countries in Asia), you may have additional rights under local law, and we will respond to valid requests within a reasonable time.

8. Security

We limit access to request information and use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission over the Internet, including email, is completely secure. Please do not include passwords or payment card numbers in your request.

9. Children

This service is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: help@elliongpu.com.
